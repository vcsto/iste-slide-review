What makes the cut?

How these ratings work

Priority measures potential impact on the application. Confidence measures support for the recommendation, not the likelihood that ISTE will approve an indicator. Only high-confidence corrections are included. Priority distinguishes substantive mismatches from minor text fixes. Ties use the other rating, then original slide number.
